About Broiler Chickens

Broiler chickens are special chickens raised mainly for meat production. They grow efficiently when provided with proper food, clean water and good management.

Feeding Broiler Chickens

Broiler chickens need balanced food for healthy growth.

  • Starter feed
  • Grower feed
  • Finisher feed
  • Clean drinking water

Chicken Housing

A good poultry house should have proper ventilation, enough space, clean bedding and protection from rain, extreme temperatures and predators.

Health Management

  • Keep the poultry house clean.
  • Provide clean drinking water.
  • Follow veterinary vaccination advice.
  • Observe chickens every day.
  • Contact a livestock officer when chickens become sick.
